\# Overview

NodiAI is a Private Document Intelligence platform that enables institutions to search, summarize, and work with internal documents using AI while maintaining control over sensitive information.

The website serves as the primary digital presence for NodiAI and is designed to educate visitors, build trust, showcase the product vision, and generate leads for pilot programs and future deployments.

\---

\# Vision

Enable organizations to benefit from AI without exposing sensitive internal information to external platforms.

Organizations should not have to choose between:

\- AI Productivity  
\- Data Control

NodiAI delivers both.

\---

\# Problem Statement

Organizations such as:

\- Schools  
\- Colleges  
\- Universities  
\- Clinics  
\- SMEs  
\- Legal Firms  
\- Government Organizations

generate and manage large amounts of internal documentation.

Examples:

\- Student records  
\- Internal reports  
\- Policies  
\- Contracts  
\- Patient information  
\- Institutional knowledge

Current challenges include:

\- Time spent searching documents  
\- Manual administrative workflows  
\- Difficulty accessing internal knowledge  
\- Concerns around uploading sensitive information to public AI tools  
\- Need for stronger privacy and governance controls

\---

\# Solution

NodiAI provides a Private Document Intelligence platform that can be deployed within an organization's controlled environment.

Capabilities include:

\- Internal Knowledge Search  
\- AI-Powered Summarization  
\- Document Question Answering  
\- Action Item Extraction  
\- Communication Drafting  
\- Assamese ↔ English Translation

Deployment options:

\- Own Server  
\- Private Cloud  
\- Controlled Infrastructure
